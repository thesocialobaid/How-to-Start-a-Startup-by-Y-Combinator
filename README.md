This repository is a comprehensive breakdown of the YC "How to Start a Startup" curriculum. It combines core notes from the original lectures with a critical look at what works and what doesn't in today's startup ecosystem.

## What Works vs What Doesn't 

One of the unique aspects of this repo is the "Field Report" section for each lecture.

✅ What Consistently Works
Talking to Users: Nothing replaces the feedback loop. "Do things that don't scale" remains the gold standard for early growth.

Building a "Painkiller": Startups that solve a burning, immediate problem (high hair-on-fire index) still find PMF faster than "Vitamin" apps.

Relentless Execution: Moving fast is the only structural advantage a startup has over a big company.

❌ What Doesn't Work (Common Pitfalls)
The "Build it, and they will come" Fallacy: Great engineering without a distribution strategy is a slow death.

Premature Scaling: Spending money on marketing before the product retention is solved.

"Coffee Shop" Networking: Distinguishing between "fake work" (networking events) and "real work" (coding and talking to users).
